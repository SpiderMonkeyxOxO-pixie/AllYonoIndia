What Does "Maha" Actually Mean in Maha Games?

"Maha" is a widely-used Hindi and Sanskrit word meaning "great" or "grand" — it shows up across countless Indian product and place names for exactly that reason: it signals scale or importance as a branding choice. In Maha Games specifically, it isn't a reference to a particular game mode, a bonus tier, or a claim that this app has more content than others in the same directory.
