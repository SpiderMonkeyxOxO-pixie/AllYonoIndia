How Rummy Is Generally Classified in India

Indian courts have long described rummy as a game of "substantial skill" rather than pure chance. That history no longer settles the question for online play involving money. The Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, 2025 and the Online Gaming Rules, 2026, in force since 1 May 2026, prohibit online money games across India, whether based on skill, chance, or both — a framework that applies to any new app, Spin Rummy included, from the moment it launches. Read our guide to the current legal position on online rummy for the official sources.

Older articles often list states such as Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Tamil Nadu, Odisha, Assam, Nagaland, and Sikkim as places where real-money rummy is restricted. Those state-level rules predate the national Act, so a state's absence from that list is not permission to play for money.
