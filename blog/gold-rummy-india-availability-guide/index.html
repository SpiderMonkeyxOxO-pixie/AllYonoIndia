How Rummy Is Generally Classified in India

Separate from anything specific to Gold Rummy: Indian courts have long described rummy as a game of "substantial skill" rather than pure chance. That history no longer settles the question for online play involving money. The Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, 2025 and the Online Gaming Rules, 2026, in force since 1 May 2026, prohibit online money games across India, whether they are based on skill, chance, or both. Read our guide to the current legal position on online rummy for the official sources.

Older articles often list states such as Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Tamil Nadu, Odisha, Assam, Nagaland, and Sikkim as the places where real-money rummy is restricted. Those state-level rules predate the national Act, so a state being absent from that list should not be read as permission to play for money. Gold Rummy's own behaviour in different states has not been independently tested.
