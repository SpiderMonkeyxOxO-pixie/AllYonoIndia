All Yono Apps Not Opening or Crashing? Common Fixes

Last Reviewed: 28 June 2026

If an All Yono-named app won't open, crashes, or freezes, the cause is usually one of a small set of common issues. This page walks through general troubleshooting steps before assuming the worst.

Common Reasons Apps Crash or Won't Open

  • An outdated APK version — if the app hasn't been updated to the latest listed file, it may no longer work correctly.
  • Low device storage — insufficient free space can cause unpredictable crashes across many apps, not just this one.
  • OS compatibility — very old or very new Android versions can sometimes behave unexpectedly with a given app.
  • Server-side maintenance — the app's backend may be temporarily down for updates, independent of anything on your device.
  • Unstable network connection — many of these apps require an active connection to function at all.

Basic Fixes to Try First

  1. Fully close the app (not just minimise it) and reopen it.
  2. Restart your device — this clears temporary memory issues more often than people expect.
  3. Check your available storage space and free some up if it's low.
  4. Confirm your internet connection is stable.
  5. Reinstall using the current link listed on All Yono Games rather than an old downloaded file.

If you reinstall, review permissions again afterward — see All Yono App Permissions Explained for what to expect.

When the Issue Is Likely on the App's Side, Not Yours

If the app was working normally and suddenly stops for many users at once, this often points to a server-side issue or scheduled maintenance rather than something wrong with your specific device. In that case, waiting and checking again later is usually more effective than repeated reinstalling.
