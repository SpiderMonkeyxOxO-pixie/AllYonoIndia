How Rummy Is Generally Classified in India

Separate from anything specific to Money Rummy: rummy has consistently been treated by Indian courts as a game requiring "substantial skill" rather than pure chance, most notably in a series of rulings going back decades. This classification is why rummy apps operate legally in most of India, in contrast to games classified as pure games of chance.

That said, a handful of states restrict or ban real-money skill gaming outright, regardless of the skill classification — including states like Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Tamil Nadu, Odisha, Assam, Nagaland, and Sikkim, where access to real-money rummy apps is commonly restricted.
