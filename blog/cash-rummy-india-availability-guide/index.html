Is Cash Rummy Legal in India?

Cash Rummy itself hasn't been individually tested against a specific legal standard — no platform gets a bespoke ruling. What's confirmed instead is the broader rule it falls under: since 1 May 2026, the Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, 2025 prohibits online money games across India, regardless of whether a given game (rummy, poker, or anything else in Cash Rummy's ten-game lineup) is classified as skill-based or chance-based. That's a national rule, not a state-by-state patchwork, and it applies the same way to Cash Rummy as it does to every other real-money app this directory tracks. For the sourcing behind that, see this site's guide to the current legal position on online rummy.

Older content elsewhere on the internet still lists specific states — Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Tamil Nadu, Odisha, Assam, Nagaland, Sikkim — as the places where real-money rummy gets restricted. Those lists predate the national Act. A state's absence from that older list isn't permission; the 2026 rule supersedes it everywhere.
