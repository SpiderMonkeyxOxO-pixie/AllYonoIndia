What "Coming Soon," "Just Launched," and "NEW" Mean on All Yono India

Last Reviewed: 24 October 2026

A label on this site describes where an app is in its own listing lifecycle — not a quality rating or a recommendation.

The Homepage Spotlight: Three Stages

Apps move through up to three stages in the homepage spotlight section before settling into the main directory:

  • Coming Soon — the app has an announced or expected launch date but isn't available yet. The card shows a countdown and a disabled download button. Spin Rummy currently carries this label.
  • Just Launched — the app has gone live within its first stretch on the platform. The countdown is replaced with a confirmed (or still-unconfirmed) download link. VIP Rummy and Jaiho Play currently carry this label.
  • Now Live (green badge) — an app that has been live long enough to move past "Just Launched" but is still called out in the spotlight section, usually with a confirmed download link already in place. Jeet Spin currently carries this label.

An app is eventually removed from the spotlight section entirely once it's established, at which point it only appears in the main directory grid below.

The Directory Grid: NEW vs Active

Every app in the main /all-yono-games/ grid also carries its own pair of status pills, separate from the spotlight labels above:

  • NEW — recently added to the directory grid.
  • Active — an established listing that's been in the directory longer.
  • DL: Available — a download link is currently confirmed and working.
  • Promo Code: Checking vs Available — whether a promo code has been confirmed for that app yet.

A game can show "NEW" in the grid while also still being "Just Launched" in the spotlight section — the two label sets track different things (recency in the directory vs. recency of the actual app launch).

Why This Matters

None of these labels are a safety or quality claim. "Active" means a listing has existed longer, not that it's more trustworthy than a "NEW" one. "DL: Available" means a link is currently working, not that it's been independently verified — see our guide on what download link availability does and doesn't prove about India availability.

All Yono India is an independent directory, not the owner, developer, or official representative of any listed game. Status labels describe listing recency only — always check the game's own page for its India-Specific Status before downloading or playing.

FAQs

Does "NEW" mean an app just launched?

Not necessarily. "NEW" describes how recently an app was added to the directory grid, which can differ from how recently the app itself launched — check the spotlight section or the app's own page for its actual launch date.

How long does an app stay "Just Launched"?

There's no fixed window stated on the site; it's based on how recently the app went live relative to other spotlighted apps.

Does "Active" mean an app is verified safe?

No. It only means the listing has been in the directory longer than a "NEW" one. Read our Confirmed vs Unverified vs Not Yet Reviewed guide for how we label verification status separately.

What happens when a "Coming Soon" app launches?

Its homepage card updates to "Just Launched," the countdown is replaced with a download link (once confirmed), and it typically gets its own dedicated page in the games directory.
