Is It Safe to Enter a Mobile Number Inside an App?

That depends on the application's own process, privacy policy, and permissions.

All Yono India cannot determine whether a specific third-party app's account process is appropriate for every user. Before entering a mobile number, users should read the app's own privacy information and understand why the number is being requested.

Good Login Habits for Users

A few simple habits can protect private account details:

  • Enter login information only where you clearly expect it.
  • Never share OTPs with another person or through a website form.
  • Do not send screenshots containing account details.
  • Avoid saving passwords on shared devices.
  • Use a different password for important accounts when possible.
  • Review app permissions before allowing access.
  • Close pages that pressure you to act quickly.
  • Read the related app's own privacy policy before entering personal information.

These habits are useful for any online account, not only for apps listed on this website.
