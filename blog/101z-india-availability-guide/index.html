How Card Games Like 101Z Are Generally Classified in India

Separate from anything specific to 101Z: Indian courts have long described certain card games as involving "substantial skill" rather than pure chance. That history no longer settles the question for online play involving money. The Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, 2025 and the Online Gaming Rules, 2026, in force since 1 May 2026, prohibit online money games across India, whether they are based on skill, chance, or both. Read our guide to the current legal position on online rummy for the official sources — the same national framework applies to 101Z.

Older articles often list states such as Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Tamil Nadu, Odisha, Assam, Nagaland, and Sikkim as the places where real-money card games are restricted. Those state-level rules predate the national Act, so a state being absent from that list should not be read as permission to play for money. 101Z's own behaviour in different states has not been independently tested.
