How Online Money Games Are Treated in India

The Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, 2025, together with the Online Gaming Rules, 2026, has been in force nationwide since 1 May 2026. It prohibits online money games across India regardless of whether the underlying game is based on skill, chance, or both — a framework that will apply to 123Rummy from the instant it launches, exactly as it applies to every other rummy app already covered in this directory. Rummy's long-standing status as a "game of skill" in Indian case law does not change this for an online format involving money or stakes. Why a Launch Date Doesn't Settle Legality
