"Seen in the app" means it was observed on the live app at launch. "Not Yet Reviewed" means we have not examined it, which is not a warning and not an endorsement. See how All Yono India verifies India-specific information for how we define these labels.

What "available in India" needs

We treat India availability as four checks:

  1. Aimed at India. Rupee amounts and local payment methods. Both are shown: rupees, and UPI, Paytm and BHIM. We have not tested a payment.
  2. A named operator and readable terms. The operator is named; the terms are not yet reviewed.
  3. A source you can verify. There is no official website or store listing yet.
  4. Support that answers. The app has a live chat, which we have not tested.

Two checks are partly met and two are open. That is why the status is Unverified, not Confirmed.

Why rupee amounts are not proof

An app can display rupees anywhere in the world. Rupee amounts tell you which currency the screen uses, not whether the operator serves users in India, accepts local payments or follows local rules. The same goes for payment names: seeing UPI, Paytm or BHIM on a screen is not the same as a payment working for users in India. Likewise, the existence of a working download link does not by itself establish availability in India.

The official source gap

At launch there is no official website and no store listing that we could find. The only way to the app we know of is the operator's referral link, which is a sponsored link, not an official page. A missing official source does not mean the app is unsafe, but it does mean you cannot cross-check what you download against an operator page. That is the main reason this stays Unverified.

Phone binding is not document KYC

The app asks you to bind a phone number. We did not record a separate identity-document step. We cannot say whether one appears later, for example before withdrawal, so our KYC field stays open. See how we verify UPI support and KYC requirements for how we decide.

Availability is not legality

Even a working app is a separate question from whether it is permitted. India's 2026 rules, in force since 1 May 2026, distinguish prohibited online money games from permissible social games, and an app that takes deposits and pays withdrawals raises that question directly. Read the legal status guide for the difference. Nothing on this page is permission to play.
