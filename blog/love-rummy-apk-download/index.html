If Something Trips You Up

  1. Link gives a dead page. The developer swapped it since you last saved it — the directory page always has the working one.
  2. Install gets stopped by your phone. That's Android flagging anything from outside the Play Store, nothing specific to this app; Settings → Security clears it.
  3. Connection drops during a hand. Expect a forfeit — there's no pausing a real-money table. Staying on one stable Wi-Fi network beats switching mid-game.
  4. OTP hasn't shown up yet. Wait roughly sixty seconds before trying again rather than requesting several in a row.

Should I check the promo code before or after installing?

Before, if you can — that way you already know whether to redeem the moment you've logged in, rather than finding out a live code expired while you were still installing.

Can I play Love Rummy alongside other All Yono rummy apps?

You can — accounts and promo pools don't overlap between apps, so running more than one at once causes no conflicts.

Most likely the hand counts as forfeited — live tables have no pause function. A steady Wi-Fi connection during play is the best way to avoid it.
