How Rummy and Slots Apps Are Generally Classified in India

Separate from anything specific to DhanGame: the Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, 2025, along with the Online Gaming Rules, 2026, has been in force since 1 May 2026 and prohibits online money games across India, regardless of whether they are based on skill, chance, or both. That applies to slots titles like DhanGame the same way it applies to rummy apps. Read our guide to the current legal position on online rummy for the official sources.

Older articles often list specific states as the places where real-money gaming is restricted. Those state-level lists predate the national Act, so a state being absent from an old list should not be read as permission to play for money. DhanGame's own state-level behaviour has not been independently tested, and this page will be updated once it is.

What "Available" Means Here (vs Legal Status)
