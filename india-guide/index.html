How All Yono India Verifies India-Specific Information

Last Updated: 28 September 2026

All Yono India exists to answer a narrower question than "what Yono games exist" — it exists to record what is actually known, for users in India specifically, about the platforms listed on this site: whether a platform is reachable and accessible from India, what its legal position looks like, whether it supports UPI, and whether it documents a KYC requirement.

Most of that is not known yet. This page explains exactly what All Yono India tracks, how a fact is allowed to move from unreviewed to reviewed, and — most importantly — what an unreviewed field does and does not mean.

The single most important rule on this page: missing information is never treated as a negative answer. "Not Yet Reviewed" does not mean unavailable, does not mean illegal, and does not mean unsupported. It means exactly what it says — nobody has checked yet.

What We Track

Every platform listed in the All Yono Games directory carries an "India-Specific Status" panel on its own page, covering five fields:

  • Availability in India — whether the platform's own accessibility for Indian users has been directly checked.
  • Legal Status — whether the platform's position under Indian law has been reviewed against a named, citable source.
  • State Restrictions — whether any state-level restriction affecting the platform has been sourced to a specific, dated, citable reference.
  • UPI / Payment Support — whether UPI support has been directly, individually observed on that platform's own payment page.
  • KYC Requirement — whether the platform's own terms or privacy policy documents a KYC requirement (never inferred from what similar apps typically do).

A sixth field, Tax / TDS Treatment, is tracked but intentionally not populated with any specific claim yet — see "What This Page Is Not" below.

How We Label What We Know

Each field can only ever hold one of a small set of allowed values, and every one of them defaults to an honest "not reviewed" state rather than a guess:

Not Yet Reviewed
Confirmed / Verified
Requires Legal/Financial Review
Mixed / Unclear (see source)

A value other than "Not Yet Reviewed" is only published once it has a citable, dated source attached. Legal and state-restriction fields additionally require the source to be a named statute, rule, or official notice — not an editorial interpretation.

FAQs

What does "Not Yet Reviewed" mean on a game page?

It means All Yono India has not yet independently checked that specific fact for that specific platform. It is not a statement that the fact is false, negative, or unfavorable — it simply has not been reviewed yet.

Does "Not Yet Reviewed" mean a platform is unavailable or illegal in India?

No. Missing or unreviewed data is never treated as a negative fact on this site. A status of "Not Yet Reviewed" for availability, legal status, UPI support, or KYC does not mean the answer is no — it means All Yono India has not completed a review of that specific point for that specific platform yet.

How does All Yono India verify India-specific information?

Each India-specific field requires its own type of evidence before it can move out of "Not Yet Reviewed" — a direct, dated check of the platform's own stated service area or payment page for availability and UPI support, a citation to a named statute, rule, or official notice for legal and state-restriction fields, and a direct quote from the platform's own terms or privacy policy for KYC. Fields are never filled in from assumptions about similar apps.

Does All Yono India provide legal, tax, or financial advice?

No. Nothing on this page or on this website is legal, tax, or financial advice. Legal and financial fields are only published once sourced and reviewed, and are always shown alongside the relevant caution and a link to the full Disclaimer. Users should consult a qualified professional for advice about their own situation.
